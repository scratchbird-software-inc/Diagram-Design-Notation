What changes, and what stays fixed?

Toolbar settings are temporary view overlays. Explicit pins, field identity, relation meaning, registered semantics and per-relation routing constraints stay intact. Page failures and routing conflicts are reported rather than replaced by an old image. Some combinations of constraints cannot be satisfied.

A disabled control is unsupported in the selected projection. Sequence views retain their participant lanes and time rows. Static SVG is available for print/export; viewport zoom does not change export geometry.

The browser receives synthetic source only. Hiding a field is not redaction. Do not deliver confidential source to unauthorized clients.
