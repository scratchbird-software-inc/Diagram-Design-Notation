Quickstart — geo is optional, and degrades visibly

This page deliberately loads only ddn-core.min.js + ddn-graph.min.js and renders a kind:geo view. Because geo is the runtime's one owner-directed optional kind, a missing ddn-geo never throws mid-page: the render succeeds with an inline placeholder box ("Map view requires ddn-geo.js") and a coded DDN-E010 entry on result.diagnostics — your page stays up and the gap is visible. Add <script src="ddn-geo.min.js"></script> after the graph bundle and the same source renders the real map. Works over file://.
