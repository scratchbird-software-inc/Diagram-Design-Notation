Charts, distributions and measurement

Statistical overlays (error bars, regression, loess)

Marks plus statistics: error bars on declared uncertainty, regression and loess lines fitted to the records.

chart.basic@1New DDN source
Open viewerOpen designerRun verifierDownload DDN

Why use it?

The overlay separates the measured fact from the fitted model — and says which is which.

When it helps

Reporting measurements with uncertainty or trend models.

How to read it

  • Points are records; the line is a model of them, named with its method.
  • Error bars are declared ranges, not computed guesses — unless the view declares the computation.

What you need

  • The records
  • The uncertainty per point (if any)
  • The fit method

Things to check

  • Do not show a fit without naming its method.
  • Do not let the line outrun the points it claims to summarise.
Scope of this diagram

Declared fits from declared data; no confidence interval is invented.

Work with the converted example

  1. Open the viewer and select regression. Inspect the rendered view and its source.
  2. Open the designer. Keep the selected profile and inspect its legal palette and property controls.
  3. Change one displayed title or an appropriate data value. Check the live update in the resulting diagram.
  4. Run the verifier and review its diagnostics before moving complete content into review.
  5. Download your edited DDN source to retain the change.

The example uses the indentation-based ddn-next-1 dialect. The language guide explains reusable definitions, dotted data rows and explicit property blocks.
