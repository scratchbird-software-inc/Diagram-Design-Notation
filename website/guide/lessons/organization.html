Hierarchies, maps and symbol libraries

Organization chart

Reporting lines as a tree: units and roles, top down, with names and titles in the boxes.

org.tree@1New DDN source
Open viewerOpen designerRun verifierDownload DDN

Why use it?

Escalation paths and span-of-control questions are answered by the shape before anyone reads a name.

When it helps

Onboarding docs, reorg proposals, RACI context (who the letters are).

How to read it

  • Each box is a role or unit; the line above is its manager.
  • Wide layers are wide spans; deep chains are slow escalations.

What you need

  • The roles
  • The reporting lines

Things to check

  • Do not draw dotted-line influence as a reporting line — mark it differently or omit it.
  • A box with two managers is a matrix claim; draw it deliberately.
Scope of this diagram

Declared hierarchy; HR-system accuracy is documented, not synchronised.

Work with the converted example

  1. Open the viewer and select org. Inspect the rendered view and its source.
  2. Open the designer. Keep the selected profile and inspect its legal palette and property controls.
  3. Change one displayed title or an appropriate data value. Check the live update in the resulting diagram.
  4. Run the verifier and review its diagnostics before moving complete content into review.
  5. Download your edited DDN source to retain the change.

The example uses the indentation-based ddn-next-1 dialect. The language guide explains reusable definitions, dotted data rows and explicit property blocks.
