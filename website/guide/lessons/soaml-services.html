What this diagram shows

Participants offering and consuming services through service contracts — the architecture of who serves whom.

Why use it?

Service landscapes rot by assumption; SoaML makes the contracts and their consumers a drawable, checkable graph.

When is it useful?

Service inventory design, API landscape reviews, consumer-provider onboarding.

How to read this example

  1. Participants are the parties; service interfaces (ball/socket style) attach contracts to ports.
  2. The choreography variant shows the message order the contract permits.

Information to gather first

  • The participants
  • The service contracts
  • Who provides and who consumes each

Boundaries and common mistakes

Declared architecture with contract-attachment validation; runtime governance is out of scope.

  • Do not draw a consumer wired to a provider without the contract between them.
  • A contract with no choreography is underspecified — the variant shows why.
