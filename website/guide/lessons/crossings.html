technique

What this diagram shows

How disconnected lines cross: a gap, a rounded jump, or a local square jump — the mark that says "not connected".

Why use it?

A crossing misread as a junction is a defect that ships; the crossing mark is the honest "no connection here".

When is it useful?

Any routed diagram where unrelated lines must cross — bus layouts, networks, wiring.

How to read this example

  1. Find the crossing: the mark (gap, bridge or square) belongs to the line that hops, and it means exactly one thing.
  2. Connected lines join at a node; crossed lines pass — the mark removes the ambiguity.

Boundaries and common mistakes

Crossing marks are presentation disambiguation; connectivity is declared in the model, never inferred from geometry.

  • Do not rely on colour alone to separate crossing lines — marks survive print and colour-blindness.
  • One crossing style per diagram; mixed marks reintroduce the ambiguity.
