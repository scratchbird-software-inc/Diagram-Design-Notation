What this diagram shows

Nodes, devices and execution environments as 3D boxes, with artifacts deployed on them and communication paths between.

Why use it?

Deployment is where architecture meets hardware; the 3D-box idiom keeps it readable.

When is it useful?

Documenting what runs on which machine or environment.

How to read this example

  1. Boxes inside boxes are nesting: an artifact has exactly one home node.
  2. Communication paths are structural links, not runtime traffic.

Information to gather first

  • The nodes and devices
  • The artifacts and their homes
  • The paths between nodes

Boundaries and common mistakes

UML deployment notation; middleware-specific models and topology discovery are out of scope.

  • Do not deploy an artifact to two homes without saying which is authoritative.
  • Do not draw network traffic as deployment.
