What this diagram shows

Functions as boxes with ICOM arrows: inputs in the left, controls over the top, outputs right, mechanisms below — decomposed level by level.

Why use it?

ICOM sides force the question most process diagrams skip: what constrains this, and what performs it?

When is it useful?

Function analysis in regulated or defence-adjacent programmes, or any "what does the system do" baseline.

How to read this example

  1. The context view is the single A-0 box; the variant decomposes it into numbered child functions.
  2. Arrows are labelled nouns; the side they enter is the role they play.

Information to gather first

  • The top-level function
  • Its inputs, controls, outputs, mechanisms
  • The decomposition

Boundaries and common mistakes

Declared model with ICOM-side validation; conformance certification to the IDEF0 standard is not implied.

  • Do not let an arrow change sides between levels without a tunnel declaration.
  • Every parent ICOM must appear (or be deliberately consumed) in the children.
