IDEF0 function model

Functions as boxes with ICOM arrows: inputs in the left, controls over the top, outputs right, mechanisms below — decomposed level by level.

idef0.basic@1New DDN source
Open viewerOpen designerRun verifierDownload DDN

Why use it?

ICOM sides force the question most process diagrams skip: what constrains this, and what performs it?

When it helps

Function analysis in regulated or defence-adjacent programmes, or any "what does the system do" baseline.

How to read it

  • The context view is the single A-0 box; the variant decomposes it into numbered child functions.
  • Arrows are labelled nouns; the side they enter is the role they play.

What you need

  • The top-level function
  • Its inputs, controls, outputs, mechanisms
  • The decomposition

Things to check

  • Do not let an arrow change sides between levels without a tunnel declaration.
  • Every parent ICOM must appear (or be deliberately consumed) in the children.
Scope of this diagram

Declared model with ICOM-side validation; conformance certification to the IDEF0 standard is not implied.

Work with the converted example

  1. Open the viewer and select context. Inspect the rendered view and its source.
  2. Open the designer. Keep the selected profile and inspect its legal palette and property controls.
  3. Change one displayed title or an appropriate data value. Check the live update in the resulting diagram.
  4. Run the verifier and review its diagnostics before moving complete content into review.
  5. Download your edited DDN source to retain the change.

The example uses the indentation-based ddn-next-1 dialect. The language guide explains reusable definitions, dotted data rows and explicit property blocks.
