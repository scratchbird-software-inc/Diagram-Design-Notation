Isometric chart treatment

The standard chart marks — bars, pies, areas, treemaps — extruded into an isometric treatment with a declared depth field.

chart.basic@1New DDN source
Open viewerOpen designerRun verifierDownload DDN

Why use it?

Presentations sometimes demand the 3-D look; doing it as a treatment over the same records keeps the data honest.

When it helps

Executive decks and posters, where the audience read the flat chart first.

How to read it

  • Heights and angles still encode the declared measure; depth is a second declared field, not decoration.
  • Read values off the flat variants when precision matters — isometric projection distorts by design.

What you need

  • The records
  • The measure and the depth field

Things to check

  • Do not compare depths across panels — the projection is per-view.
  • Never let the treatment hide a smaller bar behind a larger one without saying so.
Scope of this diagram

A rendering treatment over declared records; the data contract is identical to the flat charts.

Work with the converted example

  1. Open the viewer and select iso_bar. Inspect the rendered view and its source.
  2. Open the designer. Keep the selected profile and inspect its legal palette and property controls.
  3. Change one displayed title or an appropriate data value. Check the live update in the resulting diagram.
  4. Run the verifier and review its diagnostics before moving complete content into review.
  5. Download your edited DDN source to retain the change.

The example uses the indentation-based ddn-next-1 dialect. The language guide explains reusable definitions, dotted data rows and explicit property blocks.
