What this diagram shows

Piping and instrumentation symbols on one sheet: vessels, valves, pumps and instrument bubbles as used in process engineering.

Why use it?

P&ID reviews run on symbol recognition; a shared sheet keeps every drawing in the project on the same glyphs.

When is it useful?

Reference while drawing or reviewing process and instrumentation diagrams.

How to read this example

  1. Symbols are grouped by equipment family; each is labelled with its tag role.
  2. Instrument bubbles read by their letter codes, drawn here at working size.

Boundaries and common mistakes

Common-practice P&ID symbols for documentation; ISA/ISO certification of the set is not claimed.

  • Do not substitute a near-match symbol; P&ID glyphs carry safety meaning.
  • Keep one symbol scale per drawing.
