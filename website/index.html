Diagram Design Notation

A model-first language. Free tools.

DDN is an open-source diagram dialect and open standard proposal. Declare data, format and views separately in plain-text .ddn files, then project one model into ERDs, DFDs, flowcharts, C4 views, matrices, charts, timelines, fishbones, decision tables, wireframes and more.

The free DDN viewer, visual designer and verifier run locally in your browser without a server or account. Use, study, modify and share DDN under GPLv2 or later.
