The Diagram Design Notation standard

A proposed open standard, pre-1.0: specification chapters, governance RFCs, the EBNF grammar, JSON schemas, and the machine-readable registry. The normative source lives in standard/ at the repository root; these pages are rendered copies. DDN provides profiles/projections for well-known diagram families and claims no UML/BPMN/DMN conformance certification.
