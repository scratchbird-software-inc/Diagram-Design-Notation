The Diagram Design Notation standard

A proposed open standard, pre-1.0: specification chapters, the versioning policy, the EBNF grammar, JSON schemas, and the machine-readable registry. The normative source lives in standard/ at the repository root; these pages are rendered copies. DDN provides profiles/projections for well-known diagram families, including all fourteen UML 2.5.1 diagram families with documented exclusions (no XMI/OCL exchange, executable behavior or conformance certification).
