Reference — Distribution & replication kinds

These kinds describe how one logical dataset is spread out: partitions, shards, replicas, caches, logs, routers. The careful distinction DDN keeps here is logical vs physical — a shard is a fragment of the data; a replica is a deployed copy of it. Use these for scaling and resilience designs.

This page lists every Distribution element kind in DDN — 10 of them. Each one shows its symbol, what it means, what extra information it accepts, and a tiny working example. Every example on this page passes the tool’s own check and render steps.

Partitioning rule (partition_rule)

What it looks like. Drawn as a titled card in the Distribution family colour. Hand-drawn and neo styles redraw the same kind — style changes the look, never the meaning.

What it represents. A logical fragmentation policy with its key, method and version.

What you can add. Everything optional: partition_key, partition_rule, distribution (hash/range/list/…), replication_factor, replica_role, consistency, plus description and uid.
