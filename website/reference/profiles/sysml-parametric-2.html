Profile verification

  • Version-1 ports belong only to blocks; fields retain the bounded block member notation.
  • BDD emphasizes definitions and IBD emphasizes parts/ports/flows; the selected profile identity remains visible even when they share the renderer.
  • Version 2 adds proxy/full/conjugated port glyphs, multiplicity and bounded nested ports.
  • Item-flow endpoints must be legal ports and declared direction/typing remains intact; no propagation/evaluation is inferred.
  • Every selected constraint binds at least one endpoint, replacing the version-1 exactly-two limit. Show constraint parameters; equation text is not evaluated.
