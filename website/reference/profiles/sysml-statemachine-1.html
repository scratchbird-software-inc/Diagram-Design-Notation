Profile verification

  • Frames define composite regions with at most one initial marker per region.
  • Preserve region nesting and transition labels; do not apply the flat trace evaluator to a hierarchical diagram.
  • Add shallow/deep history, junction, choice, entry/exit point, fork/join and terminate pseudostates.
  • Choice has at least two outgoing, junction has incoming/outgoing, terminate has no outgoing; history and entry/exit need a composite region.
  • Entry/exit/do/internal compartments belong to state.state; submachine reference resolves to a different state. Time triggers retain after(...), at(...), when(...) form; guard/effect text is not executable.
  • Reuse the named base machinery while retaining this profile identity, method route, styling scope and exclusions. Renderer reuse does not import unrelated method permissions.
