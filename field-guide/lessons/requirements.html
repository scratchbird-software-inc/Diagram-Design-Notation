What this diagram shows

Requirements with id and text, wired by satisfy/verify/derive/refine relationships to the model elements that answer them.

Why use it?

A requirement with no satisfy link is a wish; a design element traced to no requirement is a luxury — the diagram shows both.

When is it useful?

Contract-driven development, compliance arguments, coverage reviews.

How to read this example

  1. Boxes labelled «requirement» carry the id and text; dashed arrows are the trace relationships, read with their keywords.
  2. A verify link must end at a test case; a satisfy link at a design element.

Information to gather first

  • The requirements (id + text)
  • The satisfying design elements
  • The verifying tests

Boundaries and common mistakes

Declared traceability with relationship-type validation; coverage is shown, not certified.

  • Do not paraphrase requirement text in the box — quote it, or the trace is to your summary.
  • Refine is not satisfy: refine clarifies, satisfy answers.
