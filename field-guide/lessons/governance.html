What this diagram shows

Who owns which data product, under which contract, with which consumers.

Why use it?

Every dataset that matters needs a name attached to it — the diagram is the accountability list.

When is it useful?

Establishing or auditing data-product governance.

How to read this example

  1. Owners are people or teams, never systems.
  2. A contract without a named consumer is a wish, not a contract.

Information to gather first

  • The products
  • Owners per product
  • Contracts and their consumers

Boundaries and common mistakes

Governance documentation; the contracts are stated, not enforced.

  • Do not list a team as owner when a person is accountable.
  • Do not let a product have zero consumers named — it is probably not a product.
